This release ships version 0.2.1 of the Archipelago world for *Star Ocean: The Second Story*
(its own release notes: docs/release_notes/0.2.1.md).

## What's new

- **Hell seeds are announced.** A seed made with `encounter_rate: 0` together with killer-move
  checks or learn levels (Skillsanity `special_only` or `insanity`) is a "hell seed" (Archipelago
  world 0.2.1): no random battles, with every level and killer-move use trained in the Fun City
  Fighting Arena, which is free on such a seed. Setup's **Archipelago** page now warns you when
  the seed you choose is one: "Hell Seed: no random battles. Killer-move levels and learn points
  are trained in the Fun City arena, which is free on this seed. Expect a very long grind." The
  game writes the same line to its log (`shattered-stars.log`) when it loads the seed. Ordinary
  seeds show and log nothing new. (0.02.50 plays a hell seed the same way, without the warning.)

## Fixes

- **A full party no longer loses a member when someone joins just for a fight** (Archipelago
  open world). With all eight places taken, a member let in only for a fight (Claude in Shingo
  Forest in Rena's game, Dias or Claude at the Lacour tournament) borrows a place and gives it
  back afterwards. When the scene had moved them meanwhile, the member put back landed on whoever
  had been moved into that place: in Shingo Forest the member in the first place when it wasn't
  Rena, at the tournament whoever moved up into the borrowed place. The game now remembers who
  borrowed the place, and everyone stays. Saves from 0.02.50 load as before.
