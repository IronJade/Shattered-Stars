# Shattered Stars 0.02.51

This release brings **the bench** to Archipelago games: in open world with character items and
in hub mode, the party grows to twelve, with four places on a bench and the camp menu's Party
Order to choose who fights. Your **starting member now leads from the party's first place**,
with the hero beside them, and the order you set stays. Setup **warns you about a hell seed**.
It also fixes items lost when you saved while they were still arriving, received items held up
by a full inventory, shut doors that let you walk off the map, a member lost from a full party,
and Dias coming out of the Lacour tournament with the tournament's levels instead of his own.

It goes with **version 0.2.1 of the Archipelago world**, which now has a release of its own:
[so2.apworld 0.2.1](https://github.com/IronJade/Shattered-Stars/releases/tag/apworld-v0.2.1).

Shattered Stars is a native Windows and Linux port, hand-written in C++20 on top of SDL3 and
OpenGL. It is not an emulator — no PlayStation code runs — and it ships no game data. You need
your own disc images to play.

**You need your own discs from one of these releases**, as a `.cue` with its `.bin`, or an
`.iso` / `.img`. Both discs must come from the same release. Disc 2 is optional until the story
asks for it (Archipelago's hub mode asks for it at New Game).

## What's new

- **A 12-member party: the bench** (Archipelago open world with `character_items`, and hub
  mode, in a seed made with the `bench` option of so2.apworld 0.2.1, on by default). Besides
  the game's eight places, four in front and four in reserve, the port keeps a bench of four
  more (places 9 to 12), so every member you haven't excluded can join. With the eight places
  full, a member who joins goes to the bench instead of taking someone's place.
  - In the camp menu's **Party Order**, L1 / R1 flips the right-hand column between the
    reserve and the bench, and you swap members in and out as you swap the front and the
    reserve (your first pick stays held across the flip). **Tactics** flips the same way.
  - Benched members keep their equipment, levels and skills, and you can manage them
    (Equipment, Skills, Tactics, Status, their Specialty settings), but they aren't in play
    until you bring them in: they can't use specialties, cast field spells or Link Combo, inns
    don't heal them, and the endings don't count them.
  - When the story needs a benched member for a fight they make alone (Claude in Shingo Forest
    in Rena's game, Dias or Claude at the Lacour tournament), they are lent for that fight and
    go back to the bench afterwards with what it gave them.
  - The hero is never benched, nor anyone who joins just for a while, and bench moves wait
    while the story holds the party (a Private Action, a party split for a scene).
  - Turn the option off when you generate for the classic eight.
- **Your starting member leads from the first place** (Archipelago open world and hub mode,
  with a `starting_character` who isn't the hero). At New Game your starting member now takes
  the first place in the party, the one you control in battle and the first in every menu, and
  the hero the second. The order you leave in the camp menu (Party Order, and Formation >
  Leader) stays: when a story scene shuffles the party, your order comes back once the scene is
  over, and your save keeps it. Seeds without a starting member play as before.
- **Hell seeds are announced.** A seed made with `encounter_rate: 0` together with killer-move
  checks or learn levels (Skillsanity `special_only` or `insanity`) is a "hell seed"
  (so2.apworld 0.2.1): no random battles, with every level and killer-move use trained in the
  Fun City Fighting Arena, which is free on such a seed. Setup's **Archipelago** page now warns
  you when the seed you choose is one: "Hell Seed: no random battles. Killer-move levels and
  learn points are trained in the Fun City arena, which is free on this seed. Expect a very
  long grind." The game writes the same line to its log (`shattered-stars.log`) when it loads
  the seed. Ordinary seeds show and log nothing new.
- **Archipelago world 0.2.1**, released on its own (see
  [Playing with Archipelago](#playing-with-archipelago)): the bench, the hell seed, Skillsanity
  checks that may hold progression, and open-world games that now always generate.
  **A seed made with the bench needs Shattered Stars 0.02.51.**

## Fixes

- **Items still waiting to be given are no longer lost when you save** (Archipelago). A check
  that gives several items (a skill guild set, a boss, a craft, a killer-move level...) hands
  them out one at a time, each with its banner. A save made while some were still waiting didn't
  record them, and the check was already done, so after loading they were gone for good. Waiting
  items are now kept with the save and come back when you load it, and nothing is ever given
  twice. Loading an older save after a game over, or after quitting without saving, now also
  gives you the items of the checks you had made since.
- **A full inventory no longer holds up everything else you receive** (Archipelago). An item
  that doesn't fit (twenty of a kind already, such as Blackberries) used to block every item
  behind it, items from other players included, until you made room. Now it steps aside with
  one banner ("Inventory full: Blackberries waiting"), everything else keeps arriving, and the
  item is given as soon as there is room (after you use, sell or craft one away). It is kept
  with your save while it waits.
- **A shut door no longer lets you walk off the map** (Archipelago open world and hub mode). At
  a door a seed keeps shut (Salva Drift behind Alen's bookcase, Salva's barricade, the Mars
  village exit, Zand's mansion, Central City and Noel's House exits, the goal's gate), the banner
  showed but you could keep walking into the doorway and out of the map, and get stuck. You now
  stop at the doorway; with the key, the door opens as before.
- **A full party no longer loses a member when someone joins just for a fight** (Archipelago
  open world). With all eight places taken, a member let in only for a fight (Claude in Shingo
  Forest in Rena's game, Dias or Claude at the Lacour tournament) borrows a place and gives it
  back afterwards. When the scene had moved them meanwhile, the member put back landed on whoever
  had been moved into that place: in Shingo Forest the member in the first place when it wasn't
  Rena, at the tournament whoever moved up into the borrowed place. The game now remembers who
  borrowed the place, and everyone stays. Saves from 0.02.50 load as before.
- **Dias keeps his own levels and gear through the Lacour tournament** (Archipelago, a Dias you
  received or started with). The tournament makes him its own Dias for his fights there (level 35
  after the first, his starting data after later ones), which used to stay: your Dias came out at
  the tournament's level with his starting equipment. The game now keeps your Dias aside and gives
  him back right after his last fight there, as he was, on the bench too if that's where he was.
  A save made between his fights keeps him too.

## Updating from 0.02.50

Unpack the new package and use it in place of the old one. Settings, memory cards, mods and the
seeds' own cards live in your user folder, so they stay as they are. If you keep your disc images
in the old package's `discs` folder, move them across.

**Seeds and runs in progress:**

| Seed made with | On Shattered Stars 0.02.50 | On Shattered Stars 0.02.51 |
|---|---|---|
| so2.apworld 0.1.0 / 0.1.1 / 0.2.0 | plays | plays, with this release's fixes |
| so2.apworld 0.2.1, without the bench | plays (a hell seed without the warning) | plays |
| so2.apworld 0.2.1, with the bench | refused ("unsupported version 4") | plays |

- **You don't need to generate your seed again.** Every seed that played on 0.02.50 plays on
  0.02.51 and gains the fixes above. Generate with so2.apworld 0.2.1 for the bench and its other
  changes.
- **The bench is on by default** in so2.apworld 0.2.1 for open world with `character_items` and
  for hub mode. Everyone playing Star Ocean in such a multiworld needs 0.02.51; to make a seed
  that also plays on 0.02.50, generate with `bench: false`.
- **Saves from 0.02.50 load as before.** A save that 0.02.51 makes while items are still waiting
  to be given, between Dias's tournament fights, or with anyone on the bench (or lent from it)
  keeps that with the save, and 0.02.50 refuses to load it ("The data could not be loaded"; its
  log says "unsupported sidecar version") rather than lose what it holds.
- When you start a New Game on a slot that already has items found or sent in the multiworld,
  they all arrive at the next connect. That is how Archipelago works and is unchanged.

## Installing

1. Unpack the package for your platform anywhere.
2. Run the setup program: `setup.exe` on Windows, `./shattered-stars-setup` on Linux.
3. On the **Discs** page, choose Disc 1 and Disc 2 with **Browse...**, or put your images in
   the `discs` folder next to the game and press **Auto-detect**. Setup checks each image and
   says which disc and release it is.
4. Press **Save & Play**.

After that, start the game directly with `shattered-stars.exe` or `./shattered-stars`.

Full details: [Installing and Setup](https://github.com/IronJade/Shattered-Stars/wiki/Installing-and-Setup)
and [Playing](https://github.com/IronJade/Shattered-Stars/wiki/Playing) on the wiki.

## Playing with Archipelago

Shattered Stars has the [Archipelago](https://archipelago.gg) client built into the game — no
separate client program is needed. This world is custom and **not yet part of Archipelago
itself**, so it has its own release here, apart from the game's, and can be updated on its own:

1. Download `so2.apworld` and `Star.Ocean.The.Second.Story.yaml` from
   [so2.apworld 0.2.1](https://github.com/IronJade/Shattered-Stars/releases/tag/apworld-v0.2.1).
2. Copy `so2.apworld` into the `custom_worlds` folder of **Archipelago 0.6.8 or newer**,
   replacing the old one. Keep only one copy: Archipelago refuses to load the same game twice.
3. Make your options YAML, either with the Archipelago Launcher's **Options Creator** or by
   hand, starting from `Star.Ocean.The.Second.Story.yaml`.
4. Generate as usual. Archipelago writes `AP_<number>.zip` into its `output` folder.
5. In the setup program's **Archipelago** page, press **Browse...** and choose that archive (or
   a loose `.apso2` seed file), pick your slot if it asks, and enter the room's address, your
   slot name and the password. Press **Save & Play**; the game connects as it starts.

To play a seed on your own, leave the address empty. A seed works with the discs of any
supported release, so the players of one multiworld can each use their own.

Details, every option, and troubleshooting: [Archipelago](https://github.com/IronJade/Shattered-Stars/wiki/Archipelago)
on the wiki.

## Known limitations

- **Hub mode leaves a few things out:** the story's scenes outside the boss chambers, the early
  gifts, the Lacour tournament, the fights the story lets you lose, the Portal of Sealing, the
  Nightmare, Flarelizard and Ghost fights, and the arena's prize. Scenes where the story splits
  the party are fought with the whole party.
- **The text the port adds for Archipelago is in English** on every release: the messages, the
  hub's questions, Port Hilton's captain, the hell seed's warning, and the short descriptions on
  the character select. French and German Archipelago messages use the game's own font, so a
  symbol it lacks in a player or item name shows as a similar character, or a space. In French
  and German shops, a very long item name may run under the Archipelago logo.
- **Excluding both Rena and Noel** with `exclude_characters` leaves an open-world or hub party
  with no healer.
- **Your leader on the field** gives way to the hero in a few places: a dash's afterimages show
  the hero, and so does a long scene until free play returns.
- **Port Hilton's captain:** taking his Hoffmann Ruins voyage in chapter 23 skips the soldier's
  lines of that first trip.
- **With skillsanity**, the Cave of Trials' learn spots still say the move was learned; the move
  itself arrives as its item.
- **Old option names.** YAML files written for so2.apworld 0.1.x that use `protagonist` or
  `drops_as_checks` need those keys renamed to `story_route` and `enemy_dropsanity`.
- **Instant text and auto-advance** are set in setup only, not from inside the game.
- **Setup can't show Chinese, Japanese or Korean letters** in a folder name (they show as
  boxes); the paths themselves work.
- **Japanese discs aren't supported yet.** Setup recognises them and says so.
- **The PAL versions' 50 Hz timing isn't reproduced, by design.** European, French and German
  discs play on the North American game logic at 60 Hz, with their own text.
- **Saves stay with their release.** Importing a save from another release isn't offered yet.
- **Compressed disc formats** (CHD, PBP) are not accepted yet.
- **No macOS build.**
- **The Archipelago world is not yet in Archipelago itself.** Until it's merged upstream, get
  `so2.apworld` and its template from this project's releases.

See [Bugs Fixed and Quirks Kept](https://github.com/IronJade/Shattered-Stars/wiki/Bugs-Fixed-and-Quirks-Kept)
for what was changed from the original and what was deliberately left as it was.
