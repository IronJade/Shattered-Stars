# Shattered Stars 0.02.51

This release ships version 0.2.1 of the Archipelago world for *Star Ocean: The Second Story*
(its own release notes: docs/release_notes/0.2.1.md).

## What's new

- **Hell seeds are announced.** A seed made with `encounter_rate: 0` together with killer-move
  checks or learn levels (Skillsanity `special_only` or `insanity`) is a "hell seed" (Archipelago
  world 0.2.1): no random battles, with every level and killer-move use trained in the Fun City
  Fighting Arena, which is free on such a seed. Setup's **Archipelago** page now warns you when
  the seed you choose is one: "Hell Seed: no random battles. Killer-move levels and learn points
  are trained in the Fun City arena, which is free on this seed. Expect a very long grind." The
  game writes the same line to its log (`shattered-stars.log`) when it loads the seed. Ordinary
  seeds show and log nothing new. (0.02.50 plays a hell seed the same way, without the warning.)

## Fixes

- **Items still waiting to be given are no longer lost when you save** (Archipelago). A check
  that gives several items (a skill guild set, a boss, a craft, a killer-move level...) hands
  them out one at a time, each with its banner. A save made while some were still waiting didn't
  record them, and the check was already done, so after loading they were gone for good. Waiting
  items are now kept with the save and come back when you load it, and nothing is ever given
  twice. Loading an older save after a game over, or after quitting without saving, now also
  gives you the items of the checks you had made since.
- **A full inventory no longer holds up everything else you receive** (Archipelago). An item
  that doesn't fit (twenty of a kind already, such as Blackberries) used to block every item
  behind it, items from other players included, until you made room. Now it steps aside with
  one banner ("Inventory full: Blackberries waiting"), everything else keeps arriving, and the
  item is given as soon as there is room (after you use, sell or craft one away). It is kept
  with your save while it waits.
- **A shut door no longer lets you walk off the map** (Archipelago open world and hub mode). At
  a door a seed keeps shut (Salva Drift behind Alen's bookcase, Salva's barricade, the Mars
  village exit, Zand's mansion, Central City and Noel's House exits, the goal's gate), the banner
  showed but you could keep walking into the doorway and out of the map, and get stuck. You now
  stop at the doorway; with the key, the door opens as before.
- **A full party no longer loses a member when someone joins just for a fight** (Archipelago
  open world). With all eight places taken, a member let in only for a fight (Claude in Shingo
  Forest in Rena's game, Dias or Claude at the Lacour tournament) borrows a place and gives it
  back afterwards. When the scene had moved them meanwhile, the member put back landed on whoever
  had been moved into that place: in Shingo Forest the member in the first place when it wasn't
  Rena, at the tournament whoever moved up into the borrowed place. The game now remembers who
  borrowed the place, and everyone stays. Saves from 0.02.50 load as before.
- **Dias keeps his own levels and gear through the Lacour tournament** (Archipelago, a Dias you
  received or started with). The tournament makes him its own Dias for his fights there (level 35
  after the first, his starting data after later ones), which used to stay: your Dias came out at
  the tournament's level with his starting equipment. The game now keeps your Dias aside and gives
  him back right after his last fight there, as he was. A save made between his fights keeps him
  too (0.02.50 can't load such a save).

## Updating from 0.02.50

- Replace the program as usual; your settings, cards and Archipelago cards stay where they are.
- Saves from 0.02.50 load as before. A save that 0.02.51 makes while items are waiting to be
  given, or between Dias's tournament fights, keeps that with the save, and 0.02.50 won't load
  it (it says the save needs a newer version) rather than lose what it holds.
- Seeds made with Archipelago world 0.2.0 still play. Use world 0.2.1 for new seeds.
- When you start a New Game on a slot that already has items found or sent in the multiworld,
  they all arrive at the next connect. That is how Archipelago works and is unchanged.
