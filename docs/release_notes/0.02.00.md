# Shattered Stars 0.02.00

Shattered Stars now plays the European (English), French and German releases of *Star Ocean:
The Second Story* as well as the North American one, in the same build: the game recognises
your discs and plays their release. This release also ships version 0.1.1 of the Archipelago
world, which fixes open-world seeds.

Shattered Stars is a native Windows and Linux port, hand-written in C++20 on top of SDL3 and
OpenGL. It is not an emulator — no PlayStation code runs — and it ships no game data. You need
your own disc images to play.

**You need your own discs from one of these releases**, as a `.cue` with its `.bin`, or an
`.iso` / `.img`. Both discs must come from the same release. Disc 2 is optional until the story
asks for it.

| Release | Disc 1 | Disc 2 | Text | Save product code |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| North American | SCUS-94421 | SCUS-94422 | English | BASCUS-94421 |
| European | SCES-02159 | SCES-12159 | English | BASCES-02159 |
| French | SCES-02160 | SCES-12160 | French | BESCES-02160 |
| German | SCES-02161 | SCES-12161 | German | BESCES-02161 |

Japanese discs (SLPM-86105 / SLPM-86106) are recognised but not supported yet.

## What's new

- **The European, French and German releases.** Each plays with its own text and data — its
  translation, fonts, menus, name entry and credits — on the North American game logic at
  60 Hz: the same gameplay that was checked against the original, at the full frame rate. On
  both discs, the European release runs every event entry of the field maps with the same
  results as the North American one. The French and German releases do too, except where the
  automated test answered a choice differently because their longer option text shifts its
  timing. All 564 battles of the game play out identically, frame for frame, in all three.
- **French and German text, checked against the originals.** Word wrap, accented letters, the
  name-entry keyboards, the camp menu, the Options pages and the rename page work as in the
  French and German originals running in PCSX-Redux. Every line of the opening laid out by the
  port matches the original's (88 of 88 in French, 84 of 84 in German), and so do the French
  battle result screen and the German Scan window.
- **Saves in each release's own format.** A save carries its release's product code, save
  signature and icon setting, so a card works with that release's console and emulators. In
  PCSX-Redux, the North American, European, French and German originals each list and load a
  save the port wrote, and the port loads a save the French original wrote.
- **Setup knows the releases.** The Discs page names each disc's release ("Disc 1, French
  release: OK"), and refuses a pair from two releases: "Disc 2 is the European release and
  Disc 1 the French one. Both discs must be the same release." A Japanese disc gets a clear
  message naming the releases Shattered Stars plays.
- **Archipelago world 0.1.1.** It fixes open-world logic errors that could make a seed
  impossible to finish, and the ship to El now waits until you hold the El passes (see
  [Archipelago](#archipelago)). One seed now works with the discs of any supported release.
- **Mods on every release.** Mod lines name places as they are on the North American disc, and
  the game translates them for the disc you play; a line whose data differs in that release is
  skipped, as before. `tools/so2_mod.py` reads the North American, European, French or German
  disc. Both sample mods pass the maintainer's verification on the European disc.

The work on the other releases leaves the North American discs as they were: every test suite,
the quick reference set and the smoke test pass on them unchanged, and their saves are written
byte for byte as before.

## Installing

1. Unpack the package for your platform anywhere.
2. Run the setup program: `setup.exe` on Windows, `./shattered-stars-setup` on Linux.
3. On the **Discs** page, choose Disc 1 and Disc 2 with **Browse...**, or put your images in
   the `discs` folder next to the game and press **Auto-detect**. Setup checks each image and
   says which disc and release it is.
4. Press **Save & Play**.

After that, start the game directly with `shattered-stars.exe` or `./shattered-stars`.

**Updating from 0.01.00:** unpack the new package and use it in place of the old one.
Settings, memory cards and mods live in your user folder, so they stay as they are. If you
keep your disc images in the old package's `discs` folder, move them across.

Full details: [Installing and Setup](https://github.com/IronJade/Shattered-Stars/wiki/Installing-and-Setup)
and [Playing](https://github.com/IronJade/Shattered-Stars/wiki/Playing) on the wiki.

## Archipelago

> **Open-world players: generate your seed again with `so2.apworld` 0.1.1.** In 0.1.0, about
> 4 in 10 seeds with `world_mode: open_world` could not be finished at all, and in almost every
> one a key could be left behind for good when the ship sails to El. Story mode, the default,
> was never affected: 0.1.1 generates story-mode seeds exactly as 0.1.0 did. A seed made with
> 0.1.0 still loads, but without the new gates below.

What 0.1.1 fixes, in open world only (the item pool, location names and ids are unchanged):

- **Two ship landings were missing from the logic.** The Lacour continent is reached only by
  the Herlie ship, which lands at Port Hilton, and Hoffmann Ruins only by the Hilton ship, which
  lands on its beach. 0.1.0 could put the Hilton Pass in Lacour Castle, or the Map 231 Pass in
  Hoffmann Ruins: places you can't reach without those very keys. 0.1.1 requires the landing's
  key for every area reached by ship.
- **The teleporter doesn't reach Expel's islands.** The old man in Fun City's arena takes you
  back to Expel, but only to the continents you can walk to or reach by the Herlie ferry.
  Hoffmann Ruins and Eluria Colony, which only the story's ships reach, could hold another
  player's progression or a key the story needs after the disc change, lost for good if you
  hadn't picked it up first. In 0.1.1 they hold only the Expel story's own keys, which you
  collect before Expel closes.
- **The ship to El waits for the El passes.** After the battle at the Lacour Front Line, the
  meeting at the Front Line starts a chain of scenes that ends at sea: once you are on
  El, the Cross and Lacour continents and Hoffmann Ruins stay out of reach until much later.
  0.1.0 could leave the El Beach, Eluria or Eluria Tower Pass back there. Now the logic puts
  those passes where you can still get them before you sail, and the game doesn't start that
  meeting until you hold all three: walking into it shows "El Beach Pass needed to sail"
  for each one you're missing, and you can go and fetch them first.
- **The teleporter needs the Synard.** Fun City lies on a different island of Energy Nede
  from Central City, so you can only reach the arena's teleporter once the Synard flies. 0.1.0
  counted on it from your first walk on Energy Nede. With the Expel Teleporter and Expel
  Progression options both on — the defaults in open world — other players' progression can
  sit in Expel until the Synard, so the ship to El now also waits for the North City, Noel's
  House and Red Crystal Passes, which the story needs before then.
- **No walking out of Central City or Noel's House too early.** The story brings you into
  both without going through their entrance. Until the story is done there (chapters 38 and 40),
  their exit asks for that area's pass ("Central City Pass needed to leave"), so you can't
  lock yourself out on an island you can't leave yet.

The fix was proven with the world's tests, Archipelago's general tests, a 96-seed batch with
no error (the 0.1.0 batch had an unreachable key in 44 of its 114 open-world slots, and an El
pass you could sail away from in 110), the late-game, teleporter and voyage proofs, and
end-to-end games against a local server.

Shattered Stars has the [Archipelago](https://archipelago.gg) client built into the game — no
separate client program is needed. This world is custom and **not yet part of Archipelago
itself**, so it ships here as a separate `.apworld`:

1. Copy `so2.apworld` into the `custom_worlds` folder of **Archipelago 0.6.8 or newer**,
   replacing 0.1.0. Keep only one copy: Archipelago refuses to load the same game twice.
2. Make your options YAML, either with the Archipelago Launcher's **Options Creator** (pick
   *Star Ocean: The Second Story* and set options with buttons and sliders) or by hand, starting
   from `Star.Ocean.The.Second.Story.yaml`.
3. Generate as usual. Each Star Ocean player gets a seed file ending in `.apso2`.
4. In the setup program's **Archipelago** page, choose the seed file and enter the room's
   address, your slot name and password. Press **Save & Play**; the game connects as it
   starts.

A seed works with North American, European, French and German discs alike, so the players of
one multiworld can each use their own release. The full end-to-end test against a local
server passed on the European and the French discs.

Details, every option, and troubleshooting: [Archipelago](https://github.com/IronJade/Shattered-Stars/wiki/Archipelago)
on the wiki.

## Known limitations

- **Japanese discs aren't supported yet.** Setup recognises them and says so. The Japanese
  release is a different build of the game, with its own changes to enemies, party moves,
  spells and text; it is planned for a later release.
- **The PAL versions' 50 Hz timing isn't reproduced, by design.** European, French and German
  discs play on the North American game logic at 60 Hz. The PAL versions scaled movement and
  the camera by 6/5 per frame to make up for their slower frame rate, and removed the clash of
  opposing elemental projectiles (which cancel or overpower each other); neither change is
  used, so the projectile clash stays on as in the North American game. The PAL versions also
  added the item spells The Scream and Judgement Day to the spells that never clash with
  another caster's; the port keeps the North American list, so on PAL discs those two can still
  clash. The credits' timing,
  which the PAL data counts in 50 Hz frames, is converted to 60 Hz. Each release keeps its own
  text, credits and rumble lengths.
- **Saves stay with their release.** The game uses the saves of the release you play.
  Importing a save from another release isn't offered yet.
- **Compressed disc formats** (CHD, PBP) are not accepted yet.
- **No macOS build.**
- **The Archipelago world is not yet in Archipelago itself.** Until it's merged upstream, get
  `so2.apworld` and its template from this project's releases, not the Archipelago Launcher's
  own world list.
- **French and German Archipelago messages** use the game's own font: a symbol it doesn't have
  in a player or item name shows as a similar character, or a space.
- **Open world: the ship to El won't sail without its keys.** The voyage to El is still one
  way until the disc change, so a key left elsewhere on Expel couldn't be fetched after it:
  0.1.0 could do this to you. 0.1.1 fixes it instead of just warning about it: the El Beach,
  Eluria and Eluria Tower Passes stay reachable before you sail, and with the Expel Teleporter
  and Expel Progression options both on (the open-world defaults) so do the North City, Noel's
  House and Red Crystal Passes. The game won't start the voyage until you hold all of the ones
  that apply, and names whichever you're still missing (see [Archipelago](#archipelago) above).
- **The curated mod list is small.** Two sample mods ship today; see the
  [Modding](https://github.com/IronJade/Shattered-Stars/wiki/Modding) wiki page to make or
  submit your own.

See [Bugs Fixed and Quirks Kept](https://github.com/IronJade/Shattered-Stars/wiki/Bugs-Fixed-and-Quirks-Kept)
for what was changed from the original and what was deliberately left as it was.
