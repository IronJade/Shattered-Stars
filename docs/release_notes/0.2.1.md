# so2.apworld 0.2.1

> **DRAFT** (ncpfix, 2026-10-26; skilllogic2, 2026-10-27). An Archipelago world release only, with
> no new Shattered Stars build. Settle the line marked **TO CONFIRM** (docs/handoff/skilllogic.md),
> check the SHA-256 sums against the files you upload, and remove this note before publishing.

Version 0.2.1 of the Archipelago world for *Star Ocean: The Second Story* fixes open-world games
that couldn't be generated with `expel_progression` or `expel_teleporter` off, and lets
Skillsanity's skill guild checks and learn levels hold progression items. It plays on
**Shattered Stars 0.02.50**, the current release; you don't need a new build of the game.

## What's fixed

- **Open world with `expel_progression` off.** With the Expel teleporter on (the default), a
  Star Ocean world alone, or with only other Star Ocean players, could not be generated:
  Archipelago stopped with "No more spots to place ... Unplaced items: North City Pass". The
  North City Pass is the first pass Energy Nede's story needs, nothing there can be reached
  before it, and with `expel_progression` off it may not wait in Expel either. A multiworld
  with other games did generate, because their locations took the pass.
- **Open world with `boss_checks` off** and either `expel_progression` or `expel_teleporter` off
  failed the same way on the Noel's House Pass, whose only possible place was the Synard boss.
  This affected 0.2.0 with the teleporter off too.

**Now**, in open world, you start with the **North City Pass and the Noel's House Pass** as
well as the Arlia and Fun City passes, unless `expel_teleporter` and `expel_progression` are
both on (the defaults). Before, only the teleporter-off games had the North City Pass at the
start.

## What's new: Skillsanity checks can hold progression

With `skillsanity`, buying a set at a skill guild and reaching a level where a killer move or
spell used to be learned are checks. In 0.2.0 they only ever held filler and useful items.
Now they may hold progression items too (keys, characters, other worlds' progression), as long
as the logic can count on you reaching them:

- **Skill guild checks** are expected once one of the set's guilds is in reach: the sets up to
  1,600 Fol right away, the 2,700-4,500 Fol sets once you've finished Hilton and the Hoffmann
  Ruins (story mode: from the disc switch; hub mode: once Fienal opens). A guild you can lose
  for good, like old Clik's, never counts, and with `expel_progression` off only the guilds on
  Energy Nede do.
- **Learn levels** count as killer-move levels do: up to level 30 from the disc switch, higher
  ones from the Final Save Point (hub mode: from the start, higher ones once Fienal opens), and
  only for members the logic can count on. A member who joins through their character item needs
  it first, so that character becomes a progression item.
- Not with `encounter_rate: 0` (no battles, so no Fol and no levels). Precis's and Opera's
  machines, the Cave of Trials' learn chests and Float still hold no progression, and **shops
  still never do**.

This also fixes a rare mix that couldn't be generated at all: open world in Rena's story with
`expel_progression`, `boss_checks` and `enemy_dropsanity` off, moves and spells as items, and
killer-move checks for the hero. There were 60 move items needing a place, and only 63 places
that could take one.

## Your seeds

- **Seeds made with 0.2.0 keep working** on Shattered Stars 0.02.50 as they are. A 0.2.0 seed
  that generated is fine to play; there is no need to make it again.
- **With the default teleporter and `expel_progression`**, or in story mode or hub mode, and
  with `skillsanity` off, 0.2.1 makes the same seed as 0.2.0 from the same options and seed
  number: the same items in the same places. Only the version number written into the seed
  changes.
- **A player with either of those options off in open world** now starts with the two passes,
  so the same options and seed number place the multiworld's items otherwise.
- **A player with `skillsanity` on**, in any mode, may get guild checks and learn levels holding
  progression, so the same options and seed number place the multiworld's items otherwise.

## Installing

1. Copy `so2.apworld` into the `custom_worlds` folder of **Archipelago 0.6.8 or newer**,
   replacing the old one. Keep only one copy: Archipelago refuses to load the same game twice.
2. Generate as usual. Your options YAML from 0.2.0 works unchanged; the template,
   `Star.Ocean.The.Second.Story.yaml`, only describes the new starting passes and what
   Skillsanity's checks may now hold.
3. Play the seed on Shattered Stars 0.02.50, as before.

Details, every option, and troubleshooting: [Archipelago](https://github.com/IronJade/Shattered-Stars/wiki/Archipelago)
on the wiki.

The game itself is Shattered Stars 0.02.50, from its own release.

The world was checked with its own tests (537), Archipelago's general tests (379), 2,400 solo
fills of open-world games with `expel_progression` off, with and without the teleporter, the
boss checks and killer-move progression, 1,500 solo fills of Skillsanity mixes, and batches of
96 seeds for each world mode and a batch of 96 solo games, all of which generated.

## Known limitations

- **TO CONFIRM.** The rare mix above can still fail to generate on its own (no other game's
  locations) with `encounter_rate: 0`: with no battles, guild checks, learn levels and
  killer-move levels hold no progression, but the moves those killer-move levels need are still
  progression items. Leave `encounter_rate` above 0, or turn off `killer_move_checks`.

Otherwise as in 0.2.0: see the [Shattered Stars 0.02.50 release notes](https://github.com/IronJade/Shattered-Stars/releases/tag/v0.02.50).
