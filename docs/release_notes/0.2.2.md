Version 0.2.2 of the Archipelago world for *Star Ocean: The Second Story* adds **tracker
support**. It plays on Shattered Stars 0.02.51 and later, like 0.2.1, and makes the same seeds from
the same options and seed number.

## What's new: Universal Tracker

[Universal Tracker](https://github.com/FarisTheAncient/Archipelago/releases) (UT) is an
Archipelago client that lists the locations you can reach right now, by the game's own logic,
from what the server says you have received and checked. It builds this world again on your
machine and asks it. Until now that only worked by chance: UT made the world again with another
random seed and your YAML, and in open world with `character_items`, in hub mode, or in story
mode with `character_items`, the party it drew was not yours, so it showed the wrong killer-move
levels, crafts and learn levels (30 to 130 locations off in our tests).

Now:

- **No YAML needed.** UT rebuilds the world from the room's own data: every option, your party
  pool and, in hub mode, your start town. Install `tracker.apworld` and **the same so2.apworld
  the room was generated with** in Archipelago's `custom_worlds` folder, open Universal Tracker
  from the Launcher and connect with your slot name. Your game can stay connected at the same
  time.
- **It is exact.** For every option mix we test (story, open world and hub mode, with and without
  the bench, every way the party can be drawn), the locations UT lists as reachable are exactly
  the ones the generator counted on, step by step through the whole game.
- **In story order.** UT's list runs through the areas in the order the story reaches them,
  Arlia first and the Cave of Trials last, then the skill guilds, learn levels, Item Creation and
  killer moves.
- **Excluded locations** (`exclude_locations`) show in UT's excluded group.
- **`/explain <location>` says what a location needs.** The world's rules are now data
  (Archipelago's Rule Builder) instead of code, so UT's `/explain` prints a location's rule, with
  what you have in green and what is missing in red; the logic is the same as 0.2.1's.
- **A room it can't track is refused, not tracked wrong.** If the so2.apworld you installed can't
  rebuild the room (a room made with a newer version of this world, for example), UT says "The
  world has informed UT that your options are incompatible", and its `/faris_asked` command names
  the so2.apworld to install.
- **Rooms made with 0.2.1** work with UT through 0.2.2 (install 0.2.2 for them): 0.2.1's logic is
  0.2.2's.
- **Rooms made with 0.2.0** work through 0.2.2 too, except two kinds whose logic 0.2.1 changed,
  which it refuses: a room with `skillsanity` on (0.2.1 let its guild checks and learn levels
  hold progression, with rules of their own) and a hell seed (`encounter_rate: 0` with killer-move
  checks). Track those with so2.apworld 0.2.0 and your YAML in UT's Players folder.

## Your seeds

- **The seeds are the same.** 0.2.2 makes byte for byte the same `.apso2` files and spoilers as
  0.2.1 from the same options and seed number (checked over the release workflow's 288 seeds).
  Only the room's slot data gains two keys for trackers, `logic_version` and `exclude_locations`,
  which the game doesn't read.
- Shattered Stars 0.02.51 plays every 0.2.2 seed; 0.02.50 every one without the bench, as with
  0.2.1.

## For developers

- `apworld/so2/ut.py`: the slot data -> option table, the checks, the refusal, the sort order.
  docs/ap/apworld.md "Trackers" and docs/ap/seed_format.md "Slot data and trackers".
- `test/test_ut.py` mirrors UT's rebuild and its fuzzer hook's sphere-by-sphere check without UT
  installed; `SO2_SLOW_TESTS=1` runs 20 seeds per mix. `tools/ap_ut_check.py` runs the real UT
  client headless against a local room (the release workflow): with Tracker_v0.3.6 and the
  packaged world, UT's in-logic locations were the world's own at every point checked.
