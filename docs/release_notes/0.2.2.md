# so2.apworld 0.2.2 (DRAFT)

> **DRAFT, not released.** Written 2026-10-27 by the agent "utworld" with the first piece of the
> tracker work (docs/plans/trackers.md B1: Universal Tracker), grown by "rulebuilder" (B2) and
> "logicexport" (B3: the logic export for PopTracker) and "storyaware" (story awareness, B6's story
> half). The map tab is still to come; the release agent finishes this page.

Version 0.2.2 of the Archipelago world for *Star Ocean: The Second Story* adds **tracker
support**. It plays on Shattered Stars 0.02.51 and later, like 0.2.1, and makes the same seeds from
the same options and seed number.

## What's new: Universal Tracker

[Universal Tracker](https://github.com/FarisTheAncient/Archipelago/releases) (UT) is an
Archipelago client that lists the locations you can reach right now, by the game's own logic,
from what the server says you have received and checked. It builds this world again on your
machine and asks it. Until now that only worked by chance: UT made the world again with another
random seed and your YAML, and in open world with `character_items`, in hub mode, or in story
mode with `character_items`, the party it drew was not yours, so it showed the wrong killer-move
levels, crafts and learn levels (30 to 130 locations off in our tests).

Now:

- **No YAML needed.** UT rebuilds the world from the room's own data: every option, your party
  pool and, in hub mode, your start town. Install `tracker.apworld` and **the same so2.apworld
  the room was generated with** in Archipelago's `custom_worlds` folder, open Universal Tracker
  from the Launcher and connect with your slot name. Your game can stay connected at the same
  time.
- **It is exact.** For every option mix we test (story, open world and hub mode, with and without
  the bench, every way the party can be drawn), the locations UT lists as reachable are exactly
  the ones the generator counted on, step by step through the whole game.
- **In story order.** UT's list runs through the areas in the order the story reaches them,
  Arlia first and the Cave of Trials last, then the skill guilds, learn levels, Item Creation and
  killer moves.
- **Excluded locations** (`exclude_locations`) show in UT's excluded group.
- **`/explain <location>` says what a location needs.** The world's rules are now data
  (Archipelago's Rule Builder) instead of code, so UT's `/explain` prints a location's rule, with
  what you have in green and what is missing in red; the logic is the same as 0.2.1's.
- **A room it can't track is refused, not tracked wrong.** If the so2.apworld you installed can't
  rebuild the room (a room made with a newer version of this world, for example), UT says "The
  world has informed UT that your options are incompatible", and its `/faris_asked` command names
  the so2.apworld to install.
- **Rooms made with 0.2.1** work with UT through 0.2.2 (install 0.2.2 for them): 0.2.1's logic is
  0.2.2's.
- **Rooms made with 0.2.0** work through 0.2.2 too. With `skillsanity` on, UT warns in its log
  that 0.2.1 changed the logic of Skillsanity's guild checks and learn levels (they may hold
  progression since, with stricter rules of their own): some of them may show up **later** than
  they really became reachable, never earlier (measured on 45 such rooms and on a player's own);
  regenerate with 0.2.2 for exact tracking. A 0.2.0 hell seed (`encounter_rate: 0` with killer-move
  checks) is refused: track it with so2.apworld 0.2.0 and your YAML in UT's Players folder.

## What's new: logic for PopTracker

The room now carries the world's logic as data (the slot data's `tracker` key: its regions,
entrances, events and every location's rule, about 4 to 35 KB), so a tracker that can't run the
world itself can still show exactly what it says. The **PopTracker pack** (`so2_shattered_stars`
1.0.0, its own release) reads it: with a 0.2.2 room its map and board squares are green when a
check is in logic and red when not, the same as Universal Tracker, for every option and seed,
entrance randomizer rooms later included. A room made with 0.2.0 or 0.2.1 has no such data: the
pack shows every check as reachable (a checklist) and its banner says so.

## What's new: story mode follows the story

Story mode's chapters need no items, so by the logic alone almost every check is reachable from the
start, and a tracker listed checks in towns the party hadn't reached yet. With Shattered Stars
0.03.00 connected to the room, the game tells the server which chapter it is in, and both trackers
now follow it:
- **Universal Tracker** lists only what the story has reached: a town's checks join the list when
  the story takes you there, Energy Nede's after the disc switch, the Cave of Trials and the
  chapter-60 scenes on Expel after the Final Save Point. It follows the chapter live, also back to an
  earlier save. `enforce_deferred_entrances: "off"` in host.yaml (UT's setting) turns it off.
- **The PopTracker pack** shows the checks later in the story in blue (and those the story has
  reached green, as before); its **Story** item turns that off, and its **Chapter** item can be set
  by hand when the game isn't connected.
- Before the game has connected, with an older Shattered Stars, and in open world and hub mode
  (whose stages are keyed) both show what the logic alone says, as before.

## Your seeds

- **The seeds are the same.** 0.2.2 makes byte for byte the same `.apso2` files and spoilers as
  0.2.1 from the same options and seed number (checked over the release workflow's 288 seeds).
  Only the room's slot data gains three keys for trackers, `logic_version`, `exclude_locations`
  and `tracker` (in story mode with the story's points: `tracker.story`), which the game doesn't
  read.
- Shattered Stars 0.02.51 plays every 0.2.2 seed; 0.02.50 every one without the bench, as with
  0.2.1.

## For developers

- `apworld/so2/ut.py`: the slot data -> option table, the checks, the refusal, the sort order.
  docs/ap/apworld.md "Trackers" and docs/ap/seed_format.md "Slot data and trackers".
- `test/test_ut.py` mirrors UT's rebuild and its fuzzer hook's sphere-by-sphere check without UT
  installed; `SO2_SLOW_TESTS=1` runs 20 seeds per mix. `tools/ap_ut_check.py` runs the real UT
  client headless against a local room (the release workflow): with Tracker_v0.3.6 and the
  packaged world, UT's in-logic locations were the world's own at every point checked.
- `apworld/so2/tracker_export.py` writes the `tracker` key (format 1: docs/ap/seed_format.md "The
  tracker export"); `tools/ap_tracker_logic.py` is the reference evaluator, and `test/test_tracker.py`
  checks it against the world's own logic sphere by sphere and on random item states for every UT
  mix (`SO2_SLOW_TESTS=1`: 20 seeds each), plus the export's shape, round trip and size. The
  PopTracker pack's Lua evaluator is tested against the reference (tools/ap_poptracker_test.py) and
  in the real PopTracker 0.36.0 (tools/ap_poptracker_smoke.py: its reachable sections were the
  world's own at every point).
- `apworld/so2/story.py`: story awareness, one source for both trackers: the story's points (the
  stages' first chapters, the Final Save Point at chapter 60 step 4000), each location's point, the
  export's `story` key, and Universal Tracker's deferred story events (`found_entrances_datastorage_key`
  = the port's `so2_story_{team}_{player}`, `reconnect_found_entrances`; only in UT's regeneration of a
  story-mode room, never with `enforce_deferred_entrances` off). docs/ap/apworld.md "Story awareness",
  docs/ap/seed_format.md "Story awareness". `test/test_story.py` checks UT's in-logic set against the
  reference evaluator's at every story point; tools/ap_ut_check.py and tools/ap_poptracker_smoke.py
  set the story key on a local server as the game does and check both real trackers follow it (UT:
  142 of 578 checks at chapter 3 up to all 578 at the Final Save Point; PopTracker: 37 green and 546
  blue at chapter 3). The release workflow's 288 seeds are byte-identical to 0.2.1's but this key.
