# so2.apworld 0.2.2

> **DRAFT** (2026-10-27): not released. Written by the agents of Phase B (utworld, rulebuilder,

Version 0.2.2 of the Archipelago world for *Star Ocean: The Second Story* adds **tracker
support**. [Universal Tracker](https://github.com/FarisTheAncient/Archipelago/releases) now
rebuilds your room exactly, without your YAML, and explains what a location needs. The room
carries its own logic, so the new PopTracker pack shows exactly what the world says. And in story
mode, with Shattered Stars 0.03.00 connected, both trackers follow the story. It makes the same
seeds as 0.2.1 from the same options and seed number, and plays on Shattered Stars 0.02.51 and
later.

It has its own release, apart from [the game's](https://github.com/IronJade/Shattered-Stars/releases/tag/v0.03.00)
and [the PopTracker pack's](https://github.com/IronJade/Shattered-Stars/releases/tag/poptracker-v1.0.0).
Get the game from [Shattered Stars 0.03.00](https://github.com/IronJade/Shattered-Stars/releases/tag/v0.03.00),
the pack from [PopTracker pack 1.0.0](https://github.com/IronJade/Shattered-Stars/releases/tag/poptracker-v1.0.0).

## What's new: Universal Tracker

Universal Tracker (UT) is an Archipelago client that lists the locations you can reach right now,
by the game's own logic, from what the server says you have received and checked. It builds this
world again on your machine and asks it. Until now that only worked by chance: UT made the world
again with another random seed and your YAML, and in open world with `character_items`, in hub
mode, or in story mode with `character_items`, the party it drew was not yours, so it showed the
wrong killer-move levels, crafts and learn levels (30 to 130 locations off in our tests).

Now:

- **No YAML needed.** UT rebuilds the world from the room's own data: every option, your party
  pool and, in hub mode, your start town. Install `tracker.apworld` and the room's `so2.apworld`
  in Archipelago's `custom_worlds` folder, open Universal Tracker from the Launcher and connect
  with your slot name. Your game can stay connected at the same time.
- **It is exact.** For every option mix we test (story, open world and hub mode, with and without
  the bench, every way the party can be drawn), the locations UT lists as reachable are exactly
  the ones the generator counted on, step by step through the whole game.
- **In story order.** UT's list runs through the areas in the order the story reaches them,
  Arlia first and the Cave of Trials last, then the skill guilds, learn levels, Item Creation and
  killer moves.
- **Excluded locations** (`exclude_locations`) show in UT's excluded group.
- **`/explain <location>` says what a location needs.** The world's rules are now data
  (Archipelago's Rule Builder) instead of code, so UT's `/explain` prints a location's rule, with
  what you have in green and what is missing in red, and the ways into its region. The logic is
  the same as 0.2.1's, rule for rule.
- **A room it can't track is refused, not tracked wrong.** Each room now says which logic it was
  made with (`logic_version`, 1). If the so2.apworld you installed can't rebuild the room (a room
  made with a newer version of this world, for example), UT says "The world has informed UT that
  your options are incompatible", and its `/faris_asked` command names the so2.apworld to install.

  game, the player icon. From docs/handoff/maptab.md.

## What's new: logic for PopTracker

The room now carries the world's logic as data (the slot data's `tracker` key: its regions,
entrances, events and every location's rule, about 4 to 35 KB), so a tracker that can't run the
world itself can still show exactly what it says. The **PopTracker pack** (`so2_shattered_stars`
1.0.0, its own release) reads it: with a 0.2.2 room its map and board squares are green when a
check is in logic and red when not, the same as Universal Tracker, for every option and seed. The
format already has room for an entrance randomizer later: named entrances, and doors that stay
unconnected until found.

## What's new: story mode follows the story

Story mode's chapters need no items, so by the logic alone almost every check is reachable from the
start, and a tracker listed checks in towns the party hadn't reached yet. With Shattered Stars
0.03.00 connected to the room, the game tells the server which chapter it is in, and both trackers
now follow it:
- **Universal Tracker** lists only what the story has reached: a town's checks join the list when
  the story takes you there, Energy Nede's after the disc switch, the Cave of Trials and the
  chapter-60 scenes on Expel after the Final Save Point. It follows the chapter live, also back to an
  earlier save. `enforce_deferred_entrances: "off"` in host.yaml (UT's setting) turns it off.
- **The PopTracker pack** shows the checks later in the story in blue (and those the story has
  reached green, as before); its **Story** item turns that off, and its **Chapter** item can be set
  by hand when the game isn't connected.
- Before the game has connected, with an older Shattered Stars, and in open world and hub mode
  (whose stages are keyed) both show what the logic alone says, as before.

The story is followed by its 24 points: the start of each of the 23 story stages, and the Final
Save Point (chapter 60, step 4000). A stage is the unit: a gift first given in the middle of a stage
shows from the stage's start.

## Older rooms

- **Rooms made with 0.2.1** track in Universal Tracker with 0.2.2 installed, exactly: 0.2.1's
  logic is 0.2.2's.
- **Rooms made with 0.2.0** track with 0.2.2 too. With `skillsanity` on, UT warns in its log that
  0.2.1 changed the logic of Skillsanity's guild checks and learn levels (they may hold
  progression since, with stricter rules of their own): some of them may show up **later** than
  they really became reachable, never earlier. Measured on 45 such rooms (29 showed fewer at some
  point, 16 the same, none more) and on a player's own; regenerate with 0.2.2 for exact tracking.
- **A 0.2.0 hell seed** (`encounter_rate: 0` with killer-move checks) is refused: track it with
  so2.apworld 0.2.0 and your YAML in UT's Players folder.
- **In PopTracker**, rooms made with 0.2.0 or 0.2.1 have no logic in them: the pack shows every
  check as reachable (a checklist) and its banner says so. The pack's story awareness needs a
  0.2.2 room too.

## Your seeds

- **The seeds are the same.** 0.2.2 puts the same items in the same places as 0.2.1 from the same
  options and seed number, with the same spoilers (checked over the release workflow's 288 seeds).
  Only the version number written into the seed changes. The room's slot data gains three keys
  for trackers, `logic_version`, `exclude_locations` and `tracker` (in story mode with the story's
  points), which the game doesn't read.
- There are no new options: a YAML from 0.2.1 works as it is.
- Shattered Stars 0.02.51 plays every 0.2.2 seed; 0.02.50 every one without the bench, as with
  0.2.1. Shattered Stars 0.03.00 also tells your trackers your map and story.

## Installing

1. Copy `so2.apworld` into the `custom_worlds` folder of **Archipelago 0.6.8 or newer**,
   replacing the old one. Keep only one copy: Archipelago refuses to load the same game twice.
2. Generate as usual, with your options YAML or the template, `Star.Ocean.The.Second.Story.yaml`.
3. Play the seed on [Shattered Stars 0.03.00](https://github.com/IronJade/Shattered-Stars/releases/tag/v0.03.00)
   (0.02.51 plays it too, without the map and story for trackers).
4. Trackers: for Universal Tracker, put its `tracker.apworld` beside this `so2.apworld`; for
   PopTracker, get the [pack](https://github.com/IronJade/Shattered-Stars/releases/tag/poptracker-v1.0.0).
   How to set both up: [Trackers](https://github.com/IronJade/Shattered-Stars/wiki/Trackers) on
   the wiki.

Details, every option, and troubleshooting: [Archipelago](https://github.com/IronJade/Shattered-Stars/wiki/Archipelago)
on the wiki.

## Known limitations

- **Story awareness follows stages, not single scenes.** A gift first given in the middle of a
  stage shows from the stage's start; Fienal's floors count from the last chapters' stage
  (chapter 58), though the party enters at chapter 60; the Final Save Point counts from the step
  before the save (chapter 60, step 4000).
- **PopTracker shows no "out of logic" (yellow).** The room's logic says what is in logic, not
  what could be done out of it.
- **Trackers update only while the game is online**: checks made offline show once the game
  reconnects and sends them.
- The rest: see the [Shattered Stars 0.03.00 release notes](https://github.com/IronJade/Shattered-Stars/releases/tag/v0.03.00).

## For developers

- `apworld/so2/ut.py`: the slot data -> option table, the checks, the refusal, the sort order,
  `LOGIC_VERSION` and when to bump it. docs/ap/apworld.md "Trackers" and docs/ap/seed_format.md
  "Slot data and trackers".
- `apworld/so2/logic.py`: the rules as Rule Builder data; `test/test_logic.py` keeps every rule a
  serialisable Rule Builder rule (docs/ap/apworld.md "Rules as data").
- `test/test_ut.py` mirrors UT's rebuild and its fuzzer hook's sphere-by-sphere check without UT
  installed; `SO2_SLOW_TESTS=1` runs 20 seeds per mix. `tools/ap_ut_check.py` runs the real UT
  client headless against a local room (the release workflow).
- `apworld/so2/tracker_export.py` writes the `tracker` key (format 1: docs/ap/seed_format.md "The
  tracker export"); `tools/ap_tracker_logic.py` is the reference evaluator, and `test/test_tracker.py`
  checks it against the world's own logic sphere by sphere and on random item states for every UT
  mix (`SO2_SLOW_TESTS=1`: 20 seeds each), plus the export's shape, round trip and size. The
  PopTracker pack's Lua evaluator is tested against the reference (tools/ap_poptracker_test.py) and
  in the real PopTracker 0.36.0 (tools/ap_poptracker_smoke.py).
- `apworld/so2/story.py`: story awareness, one source for both trackers: the story's points, each
  location's point, the export's `story` key, and Universal Tracker's deferred story events
  (`found_entrances_datastorage_key` = the port's `so2_story_{team}_{player}`,
  `reconnect_found_entrances`; only in UT's regeneration of a story-mode room, never with
  `enforce_deferred_entrances` off). docs/ap/apworld.md "Story awareness", docs/ap/seed_format.md
  "Story awareness". `test/test_story.py` checks UT's in-logic set against the reference
  evaluator's at every story point; tools/ap_ut_check.py and tools/ap_poptracker_smoke.py set the
  story key on a local server as the game does and check both real trackers follow it (UT: 142 of
  578 checks at chapter 3 up to all 578 at the Final Save Point; PopTracker: 37 green and 546 blue
  at chapter 3).
