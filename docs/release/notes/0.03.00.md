# Shattered Stars 0.03.00

> **DRAFT** (2026-10-27): not released. Left for release time (docs/release/release_checklist.md

This release brings **tracker support** to Archipelago games. While you are connected, the game
tells the server which map you're on, where you stand on the world map, and how far the story has
got. Trackers such as [Universal Tracker](https://github.com/FarisTheAncient/Archipelago/releases)
and [PopTracker](https://github.com/black-sliver/PopTracker) read it: they switch to the map
you're on, and in story mode they show only what the story has reached. The game also draws the
PopTracker pack's maps from your own disc, every area and every room, for PopTracker and for
Universal Tracker's map tab, so the pack itself ships none of the game's pictures.

It goes with two releases of their own:
[so2.apworld 0.2.2](https://github.com/IronJade/Shattered-Stars/releases/tag/apworld-v0.2.2), the
Archipelago world (Universal Tracker without your YAML, the logic for PopTracker, story awareness),
and the new [PopTracker pack 1.0.0](https://github.com/IronJade/Shattered-Stars/releases/tag/poptracker-v1.0.0).

Shattered Stars is a native Windows and Linux port, hand-written in C++20 on top of SDL3 and
OpenGL. It is not an emulator — no PlayStation code runs — and it ships no game data. You need
your own disc images to play.

**You need your own discs from one of these releases**, as a `.cue` with its `.bin`, or an
`.iso` / `.img`. Both discs must come from the same release. Disc 2 is optional until the story
asks for it (Archipelago's hub mode asks for it at New Game).

| Release | Disc 1 | Disc 2 | Text |
|---|---|---|---|
| North American | SCUS-94421 | SCUS-94422 | English |
| European | SCES-02159 | SCES-12159 | English |
| French | SCES-02160 | SCES-12160 | French |
| German | SCES-02161 | SCES-12161 | German |

Japanese discs (SLPM-86105 / SLPM-86106) are recognised but not supported yet.

## What's new

- **Trackers follow your game** (Archipelago, every world mode). While the game is connected to
  its room, it keeps two values up to date in the server's data storage, which any tracker
  connected to your slot can read:
  - the map you're on, and on the world maps of Expel and Energy Nede your position there, so a
    tracker can switch to that map's tab and draw you on it;
  - the story's chapter and step, so a tracker can tell what the story has reached. Universal
    Tracker with so2.apworld 0.2.2 and the PopTracker pack use it in story mode, where the logic
    alone puts nearly every check in reach from the start.

  They are updated when they change, at most twice a second, and again every time the game
  (re)connects, so a tracker that connects later reads the current values at once. Playing
  offline, without a server, with another room's seed, or with Archipelago off, the game writes
  nothing, and nothing goes into your saves. The keys and their values, for tracker and pack
  authors: [data storage](https://github.com/IronJade/Shattered-Stars/blob/main/docs/apworld/data_storage.md).
- **Maps for PopTracker, from your own disc.** The PopTracker pack ships only plain stand-in
  maps: the project publishes nothing from the game. The game draws the real ones from your disc
  1, once, into the folder PopTracker reads for the pack:
  - one map per area that holds a chest, gift, boss or recruitment (66 of them), made of that
    area's rooms side by side, each with its name above it;
  - every room on its own (794 of them: every room the doors of those areas lead to, the houses,
    shops and dungeon rooms without a check included), with its map number and name above it and
    a small lettered box beside each doorway (A, B, C...), so you can follow a path through a
    town or a dungeon room by room. Where a door leads isn't painted on: the tracker shows that,
    so it stays right if doors are ever shuffled;
  - the two world maps, Expel and Energy Nede, seen from above.

  In PopTracker, a line of the **Doors** panel beside the map ("A -> 412 Salva Drift"), or the
  **Door** box in the popup when you hover a doorway's lettered marker, opens the room it leads to; with the game connected, the
  tabs follow you room by room, not just area by area. Universal Tracker's map tab follows your
  current map and shows a marker on the world maps, but has no doors (they carry no AP location,
  entrance or event for it to track). It shows your rendered maps too: see below.

  The pack puts its check squares in the same places, so they land on the chests and on the
  people who hand things out. 33 more rooms have real names now (houses by who lives there, inn
  rooms, castle halls), each taken from the game's own data.

  Two ways to draw them:
  - The setup program's **Archipelago** page has a **Render tracker maps for PopTracker** button
    (under **Trackers**). It shows its progress as it goes ("150 of 862 images"), and setup stays
    usable meanwhile; at the end it says where the images went, with **Open folder**, and where
    the zip for Universal Tracker is, with **Copy path**. Closing setup stops it, keeping the
    images made so far (press the button again to finish).
  - From a terminal: `shattered-stars --render-tracker-maps` (on Windows `shattered-stars.exe
    --render-tracker-maps`). To put them somewhere else, give a folder:
    `shattered-stars --render-tracker-maps "C:\some\folder"`. `--ut-zip FILE` puts the
    Universal Tracker zip elsewhere, `--no-ut-zip` leaves it out.

  Either way it writes 862 PNG images, about 135 MB, in about 20 seconds on the development
  machine, then a zip of them for Universal Tracker, and nothing else (no settings, no saves); it
  needs no seed. The images go into PopTracker's user-override folder for the pack,
  `PopTracker\user-override\so2_shattered_stars` in your user folder if PopTracker keeps its
  folder there (it does once it has installed a pack), else in your Documents folder. PopTracker
  uses them in place of the pack's own. Universal Tracker can't read that folder, so the zip,
  `so2_shattered_stars_ut.zip` (about 135 MB), goes right beside it, in
  `PopTracker\user-override`: choose it when Universal Tracker asks for the pack (it holds the
  boards and the world-map marker too). Run it again after updating the pack, in case its maps
  changed. These pictures are made from your copy of the game for your own use: don't share them
  or the zip. The layout, for pack authors:
  [tracker maps](https://github.com/IronJade/Shattered-Stars/blob/main/docs/trackers/tracker_maps.md).
- **Archipelago world 0.2.2 and the PopTracker pack 1.0.0**, released on their own (see
  [Playing with Archipelago](#playing-with-archipelago) and [Trackers](#trackers)).

## Fixes

- **Checks you made offline reach the server when you continue that save online** (Archipelago).
  The game connects at the title, before you pick Continue, so a check made offline and saved was
  sent only after a later reconnect; loading a save while connected now sends its checks (and the
  goal) at once.

## Updating from 0.02.51

Unpack the new package and use it in place of the old one. Settings, memory cards, mods and the
seeds' own cards live in your user folder, so they stay as they are. If you keep your disc images
in the old package's `discs` folder, move them across.

**Seeds and runs in progress:**

| Seed made with | On Shattered Stars 0.02.51 | On Shattered Stars 0.03.00 |
|---|---|---|
| so2.apworld 0.1.0 / 0.1.1 / 0.2.0 / 0.2.1 | plays | plays; trackers see your map and story |
| so2.apworld 0.2.2 | plays; trackers get no map or story from the game | plays; trackers see your map and story |

- **You don't need to generate your seed again** for the tracker keys: they work with every seed,
  from the moment 0.03.00 connects to the room. For the logic in PopTracker and its story
  awareness, the room must be made with so2.apworld 0.2.2; Universal Tracker also tracks rooms
  made with 0.2.0 and 0.2.1, with 0.2.2 installed (see the world's and the pack's notes).
- **Saves from 0.02.51 load as before**, and 0.03.00's saves are the same as 0.02.51's: the tracker
  keys add nothing to them.
- Trackers update only while the game is online. Checks made while you play offline reach the
  server, and your tracker, when the game reconnects; so do your map and story.

## Installing

1. Unpack the package for your platform anywhere.
2. Run the setup program: `setup.exe` on Windows, `./shattered-stars-setup` on Linux.
3. On the **Discs** page, choose Disc 1 and Disc 2 with **Browse...**, or put your images in
   the `discs` folder next to the game and press **Auto-detect**. Setup checks each image and
   says which disc and release it is.
4. Press **Save & Play**.

After that, start the game directly with `shattered-stars.exe` or `./shattered-stars`.

Full details: [Installing and Setup](https://github.com/IronJade/Shattered-Stars/wiki/Installing-and-Setup)
and [Playing](https://github.com/IronJade/Shattered-Stars/wiki/Playing) on the wiki.

## Playing with Archipelago

Shattered Stars has the [Archipelago](https://archipelago.gg) client built into the game — no
separate client program is needed. This world is custom and **not yet part of Archipelago
itself**, so it has its own release here, apart from the game's, and can be updated on its own:

1. Download `so2.apworld` and `Star.Ocean.The.Second.Story.yaml` from
   [so2.apworld 0.2.2](https://github.com/IronJade/Shattered-Stars/releases/tag/apworld-v0.2.2).
2. Copy `so2.apworld` into the `custom_worlds` folder of **Archipelago 0.6.8 or newer**,
   replacing the old one. Keep only one copy: Archipelago refuses to load the same game twice.
3. Make your options YAML, either with the Archipelago Launcher's **Options Creator** or by
   hand, starting from `Star.Ocean.The.Second.Story.yaml`.
4. Generate as usual. Archipelago writes `AP_<number>.zip` into its `output` folder.
5. In the setup program's **Archipelago** page, press **Browse...** and choose that archive (or
   a loose `.apso2` seed file), pick your slot if it asks, and enter the room's address, your
   slot name and the password. Press **Save & Play**; the game connects as it starts.

To play a seed on your own, leave the address empty. A seed works with the discs of any
supported release, so the players of one multiworld can each use their own.

## Trackers

Trackers connect to the same room and slot as the game, next to it (they need the room's
password too). They only listen: the server takes no checks from a tracker.

- **Universal Tracker** (0.3.6 or later): put its `tracker.apworld` and **the room's
  `so2.apworld`** (0.2.2 for a room made with 0.2.0, 0.2.1 or 0.2.2) into Archipelago's
  `custom_worlds` folder, open **Universal Tracker** from the Launcher and connect with your slot
  name. No YAML is needed. `/explain <location>` says what a location needs. For its map tab it asks
  once for a zip of pictures: `so2_shattered_stars_ut.zip` from the render (above: your own maps)
  or the PopTracker pack's zip (placeholders).
- **PopTracker** (0.36.0 or later): drag `so2_shattered_stars_1.0.0.zip` from the
  [pack's release](https://github.com/IronJade/Shattered-Stars/releases/tag/poptracker-v1.0.0)
  into PopTracker's window, load **Star Ocean: The Second Story (Shattered Stars)**, and connect
  with the **AP** button. For the real maps, press setup's **Render tracker maps for PopTracker**
  once (above).

Both show what you have received and checked as the server knows it, so they update while the
game is online. Details and troubleshooting: [Trackers](https://github.com/IronJade/Shattered-Stars/wiki/Trackers)
on the wiki; every option: [Archipelago](https://github.com/IronJade/Shattered-Stars/wiki/Archipelago).

## Known limitations

- **Trackers update only while the game is online.** Checks you make offline (or while the
  connection is down) reach the server, and your trackers, when the game reconnects; the map tab
  and the story position follow from then on too.
- **Setup's render button always writes to PopTracker's usual folder.** For a portable PopTracker
  (a `portable.txt` beside it), which reads overrides only from its own folder, give the folder on
  the command line: `shattered-stars.exe --render-tracker-maps
  "<PopTracker's folder>\user-override\so2_shattered_stars"`.
- **The rendered maps are still pictures**: each room at a fixed moment, its animations at their
  first frame.
- **Hub mode leaves a few things out:** the story's scenes outside the boss chambers, the early
  gifts, the Lacour tournament, the fights the story lets you lose, the Portal of Sealing, the
  Nightmare, Flarelizard and Ghost fights, and the arena's prize. Scenes where the story splits
  the party are fought with the whole party.
- **The text the port adds for Archipelago is in English** on every release: the messages, the
  hub's questions, Port Hilton's captain, the hell seed's warning, and the short descriptions on
  the character select. French and German Archipelago messages use the game's own font, so a
  symbol it lacks in a player or item name shows as a similar character, or a space. In French and
  German shops, a very long item name may run under the Archipelago logo.
- **The bench's join banner** reads "joined the party  bench": the game's font has no
  parentheses.
- **Excluding both Rena and Noel** with `exclude_characters` leaves an open-world or hub party
  with no healer.
- **Your leader on the field** gives way to the hero in a few places: a dash's afterimages show
  the hero, and so does a long scene until free play returns.
- **Port Hilton's captain:** taking his Hoffmann Ruins voyage in chapter 23 skips the soldier's
  lines of that first trip.
- **With skillsanity**, the Cave of Trials' learn spots still say the move was learned; the move
  itself arrives as its item.
- **Old option names.** YAML files written for so2.apworld 0.1.x that use `protagonist` or
  `drops_as_checks` need those keys renamed to `story_route` and `enemy_dropsanity`.
- **Instant text and auto-advance** are set in setup only, not from inside the game.
- **Setup can't show Chinese, Japanese or Korean letters** in a folder name (they show as
  boxes); the paths themselves work.
- **Japanese discs aren't supported yet.** Setup recognises them and says so.
- **The PAL versions' 50 Hz timing isn't reproduced, by design.** European, French and German
  discs play on the North American game logic at 60 Hz, with their own text.
- **Saves stay with their release.** Importing a save from another release isn't offered yet.
- **Compressed disc formats** (CHD, PBP) are not accepted yet.
- **No macOS build.**
- **The Archipelago world is not yet in Archipelago itself**, and the PopTracker pack is not in
  PopTracker's own pack list yet: get both from this project's releases.

See [Bugs Fixed and Quirks Kept](https://github.com/IronJade/Shattered-Stars/wiki/Bugs-Fixed-and-Quirks-Kept)
for what was changed from the original and what was deliberately left as it was.
