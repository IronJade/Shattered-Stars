# Shattered Stars 0.02.50

This release ships version 0.2.0 of the Archipelago world, its first major revision: a new
**hub mode** that opens the whole game at once, item creation, killer moves, skills and spells
as checks, three goals, three difficulty settings, a character select and a reworked open-world
party. The port gains the setup side to go with it: seeds straight from Archipelago's archive,
a memory card for each seed, an on / off switch, and settings for instant text and
auto-advance. It also fixes saving on Windows for players whose folders have accented names,
and such names on a command line, a weapon graphic, a soft-lock in open-world seeds and several
smaller faults.

Shattered Stars is a native Windows and Linux port, hand-written in C++20 on top of SDL3 and
OpenGL. It is not an emulator — no PlayStation code runs — and it ships no game data. You need
your own disc images to play.

**You need your own discs from one of these releases**, as a `.cue` with its `.bin`, or an
`.iso` / `.img`. Both discs must come from the same release. Disc 2 is optional until the story
asks for it (Archipelago's hub mode asks for it at New Game).

| Release | Disc 1 | Disc 2 | Text |
|---|---|---|---|
| North American | SCUS-94421 | SCUS-94422 | English |
| European | SCES-02159 | SCES-12159 | English |
| French | SCES-02160 | SCES-12160 | French |
| German | SCES-02161 | SCES-12161 | German |

Japanese discs (SLPM-86105 / SLPM-86106) are recognised but not supported yet.

## What's new

- **Archipelago world 0.2.0**, with a hub mode, craftsanity, killer-move checks, skillsanity,
  goals, difficulty and the open-world party (see [Archipelago world 0.2.0](#archipelago-world-020)).
  **Seeds made with it need Shattered Stars 0.02.50.**
- **Seeds straight from the archive.** Setup's **Archipelago** page takes the `AP_<number>.zip`
  that Archipelago writes, as it is: there is no need to unpack it. **Browse...** starts in
  Archipelago's own `output` folder. When the archive holds more than one Star Ocean player, a
  **Your slot** list lets you pick yours.
- **A memory card for each seed.** A seed now saves on a card of its own, kept in an
  `archipelago` folder beside your settings, never on your ordinary cards. Your own saves can't
  be loaded into a seed, and a seed can't overwrite them. The Archipelago page shows how many
  saves the seed has, with **Open folder** to find them.
- **Archipelago on or off.** A switch on the **Archipelago** row of setup's sidebar turns it off:
  you play the original game on your own cards, and the seed and server stay filled in for
  next time.
- **Saves that stay with their seed.** With a seed, only that seed's own saves load. A save
  from another seed, an ordinary save, or a save changed outside the game is refused with the
  game's own "The data could not be loaded" message. This guards against accidents and casual
  save editing; it is not meant as cheat protection.
- **No debugging in an Archipelago game.** A game played with a seed ignores the debugging
  options (the debug console, map and battle starts, and the like), and its log says which ones
  it ignored.
- **Dialogue at your pace.** Setup has a new **Gameplay** page with **Instant text** (each
  message page appears at once) and **Auto-advance** (pages move on by themselves after a wait
  of 0.5 to 3 seconds, but never past a choice; the battle result screen too). Both are off by
  default and work in every game, with or without Archipelago. The game itself plays exactly as
  before.
- **The story route skips its screens.** When a seed was made for Claude's or Rena's story,
  New Game skips the hero select and the naming screen and starts that hero's game, with the
  default name.
- **An icon and a name of its own.** Both programs show an icon (an original design) and their
  names, "Shattered Stars" and "Shattered Stars Setup", in the title bar, the taskbar and Task
  Manager (which called the game "POSIX WinThreads for Windows" before). On Linux,
  `install-desktop-entry.sh` adds both to the application menu, so the taskbar shows them on
  GNOME and KDE Wayland too.
- **Esc asks before quitting.** Esc no longer closes the game at once: it pauses the game and
  asks "Quit Shattered Stars?", with **Keep playing** highlighted. Left / Right choose, Enter
  (or Cross on the gamepad, as you have it bound) picks, and Esc again goes back to the game,
  exactly where it was. The window's close button and Alt+F4 still close it straight away.
- **Fixes**, among them saving on Windows, Claude's sword in Rena's game and a soft-lock in
  open-world seeds (see [Fixes](#fixes)).

## Archipelago world 0.2.0

### Hub mode

`world_mode: hub` is a new way to play. New Game starts at the end of the story's journey, after
the Final Save Point on disc 2, with both worlds open, the Synard parked beside you, and every
location still to find. From there the game is yours to take in any order:

- **Dungeons open with their keys.** Each dungeon has a key ("Salva Drift Key", "Cave of Red
  Crystal Key", ...), found in the multiworld; a locked entrance names the key it needs. A
  dungeon with nothing behind it needs no key. The towns, their shops and their chests are open
  from the start.
- **Every story boss waits in its chamber.** 22 bosses, from Alen-Tax to Berle, play their
  original scene and fight where the story put them, and each win is a check. Three of them
  wait in towns: Berle in Armlock, Shigeo in Fun City and Marsilio in the arena.
- **Shigeo and Marsilio ask first.** Shigeo's street is on the way from Energy Nede back to
  Expel, so walking into it asks "Face Shigeo now?". Say no and you walk on through; he asks
  again on your next visit, once you've been back on the world map. The arena's desk offers
  "Face Marsilio." beside "Register.", so the ordinary arena stays open for training.
- **Fienal opens on the bosses.** Its gate needs no key: it opens once you've beaten the bosses
  `fienal_requirement` names, and shows which ones are left ("Defeat Magichand first").
  Indalecio waits inside.
- **The story stays where it is.** Scenes that would move the story on are held back, and
  nothing closes behind you: no disc change, no one-way voyage.
- **Start where you like.** `hub_start` picks Fun City (the default), Arlia, or a random one of
  16 towns on either world. Crossing between the worlds needs no key: the arena's old man in Fun
  City and the guide in Arlia take you across.
- **Characters are items** in hub mode; nobody joins through the story.

Each of the 22 boss chambers was played through in both heroes' games, all 16 starts were tested
in both, a search of the whole hub game confirmed what each seed can reach, and a 96-seed batch
and an end-to-end game against a local server passed.

### New options

| Option | Values (default first) | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `world_mode` | `story`, `open_world`, **`hub`** | Hub mode is new (above). |
| `hub_start` | `fun_city`, `arlia`, `random_town` | Where a hub game starts. |
| `fienal_requirement` | `fields_and_berle`, `fields`, `all_bosses` | What opens Fienal in a hub game: the four Fields' bosses and Berle, the Fields only, or every story boss outside Fienal. |
| `goal` | `indalecio`, `indalecio_limiter_off`, `indalecio_limiter_off_and_iseria_queen` | Beat Indalecio as before (`final_boss` still works), or his stronger form after Filia removes the limiter in Central City, or that and the Iseria Queen too. The way to Indalecio stays shut, with a message, until the goal's conditions are met. |
| `difficulty` | `earth`, `galaxy`, `universe`, `any` | The Battle Rank. New Game sets it and skips the screen; `any` shows the screen with all three offered. Only the monsters' strength changes, so the logic is the same. |
| `craftsanity` | off, on | The first time you make each item with Item Creation is a check: 553 of them, Publishing's books included. You keep what you made. |
| `no_fail_crafting` | off, on | Item Creation never fails by chance. |
| `killer_move_checks` | off, on | Each proficiency level of a killer move is a check: 133 of them. |
| `killer_move_progression` | `never`, `hero`, `any` | Whether those checks may hold progression: never, only the hero's moves, or any member's. |
| `skillsanity` | `none`, `skills_only`, `special_only`, `insanity` | Skills, or killer moves and spells, or both, become items. Buying a guild's skill set gives a check for each skill in it, and each point where a member would learn a move or spell is a check (148). With moves and spells as items, a member starts with only their first move and learns the rest as they arrive. |
| `skillsanity_shop_share` | 10 (0-100) | With skillsanity and shop shuffling, the share of skill and move items sold in shops as themselves. |
| `drop_progression` | `allow`, `force`, `never` | With `enemy_dropsanity`, whether an enemy's first drop may hold progression. Only enemies you can always meet again, and only drops with fair odds, ever do. |
| `area_keys_in_drops` | on, off | Whether open-world area keys may be in drops. |
| `starting_character` | `hero`, `any`, or a name | Open world and hub: who joins at the start and leads the party. `any` lets you pick at New Game, on a new character select. |
| `exclude_characters` | none | Open world and hub: members who never join. |

**Renamed options.** `protagonist` is now `story_route`, and `drops_as_checks` is now
`enemy_dropsanity`. **Rename them in your old YAML files**: Archipelago ignores the old names,
with a warning, and uses the defaults instead (`any` and `off`).

### What else changed

- **The open-world party** (open world with `character_items`): no member locks out another any
  more, Leon and Dias join in either hero's game, the other hero and Noel can be items too, and
  if your party could end up with no healer, the world makes sure Rena or Noel is in it. The
  story's own party splits still happen, and members set aside come back when they're over.
- **Your leader walks the field.** In open world and hub mode, the Formation leader you choose
  in the camp menu (your starting character, from the start) is the one you see on field maps,
  and the story's lines call them by name, in the hub's boss scenes too. The hero comes back on
  the world map and for the scenes that need them. This is only how it looks: the story plays
  as it would.
- **New members join at the party's level.** In open world and hub mode, a member who joins
  starts at the middle level of the party, not the level the story would give them.
- **Port Hilton's captain** (open world): the sailor offers the voyages open to you right now —
  Cross, Hoffmann Ruins, El (when the story is ready to sail and you hold its passes), or "Not
  now". Ships check their landing's key before taking your fare.
- **Shop slots give what you buy.** With `shuffle_shops`, the first purchase of a slot now gives
  you everything you bought and the slot's check as well. A slot still to check has the
  Archipelago logo after its name. This works for existing seeds too.
- **Gift sites never hold progression**, in any world mode.
- **The Cave of Trials by its real floors.** Its locations were all named "Cave of Trials Level
  1: ..."; they now carry their real floor ("Cave of Trials Level 7: ..."), and the treasure
  vault and the Weird Beast's arena have their own names. Three boss names were corrected the
  same way (Phoenix is on floor 12, G-Celesta on 13, Weirdbeast in its arena). The location ids
  are unchanged.
- **Three Cave of Trials chests are gone.** "Portrait F / G / J chest" in 0.1.x were not chests
  but the spots that teach Leon Extinction, Ashton tri-Ace and Celine Meteor Swarm. They are
  no longer locations (with skillsanity they come back as learn points), so a seed with
  `cave_of_trials` has three locations fewer.

The world was proven with its own tests (380), Archipelago's general tests (379), batches of 96
seeds for each world mode with no error, the late-game and hub proofs, and end-to-end games
against a local server, both in story and open world and in hub mode.

## Fixes

- **Saving on Windows.** When the folder holding the memory card had a name with letters
  outside plain English — a Windows user name such as "José" or "Jürgen", or an Archipelago
  slot name like "Cléo" — saving seemed to work but wrote nothing, and the save was gone the
  next time the game started. Saves now land wherever the card lives. Linux was not affected.
- **Accented names on the Windows command line.** Starting the game or setup from a command
  line or a shortcut with such a path, for example `--memcard1 "C:\Games\Test Ä\card.mcd"`,
  stopped both at once ("Cannot convert character sequence"). Every path on the command line
  and in the `SS_` variables now works whatever its letters, and the console shows them as
  written. Playing through setup was never affected.
- **The log keeps the Archipelago lines.** `shattered-stars.log`, beside `config.ini` (the file
  to attach to a bug report), now holds every line the console shows: the seed, checks, items,
  saves and loads, and the server's messages. It stays under 4 MB however long you play.
- **Archipelago servers** now see the game as a client of Archipelago 0.6.8, the version this
  world needs, instead of 0.6.6.
- **Setup's Auto-detect**, with Disc 1 already chosen, found Disc 2 but said "Found Disc 2, but
  no Disc 1." It now says it found Disc 2 next to your Disc 1.
- **A debug line**, the original game's "pwnd->destroy", no longer appears each time a menu
  window closes.
- **Claude's sword in Rena's game.** A party member who fights with a weapon drew the wrong,
  stretched weapon picture whenever they weren't first in the party: Claude in Rena's game, for
  one, from the Alen-Tax fight on. Every weapon user now draws their own weapon, wherever they
  stand.
- **Open world: stuck on the Lacour continent.** In chapters 23 and 24, Port Hilton's sailor —
  the only way off the continent — refused to sail, while the seed could have put the keys you
  needed next on Cross. He now takes you. This affected open-world seeds from so2.apworld 0.1.0
  and 0.1.1; the new port alone repairs them, with no new seed needed.
- **Archipelago shops.** Buying a shop slot gave the check instead of what you bought; it now
  gives both (above). A slot whose check was Fol lost that Fol when the purchase was paid; it now
  arrives. The mark for a slot still to check never showed in 0.02.00; it does now.
- **The Cave of Trials' learn chests in 0.1.x seeds** read and wrote past the party's records.
  Now nothing is changed: opening one gives or sends the item its location holds.
- **Courier orders in Archipelago games.** An order that would take an item past 20 was lost
  entirely, a fault of the original game. With a seed, the copies that fit are delivered and the
  rest wait for you as received items. Without a seed, the original's behaviour is kept.
- **Two story scenes** — the Field of Courage before its Guardian, and Eluria Tower before Berle
  — left the script engine one value out of step after the next battle, a fault of the original
  game that could crash the port later. Fixed; nothing you see or hear changes.
- **Menus** read past the end of their window graphics each time they opened. Nothing visible
  changes, but a possible crash is gone.
- **The debug console** (`--debug`) could leave the game hanging as it quit, when started from
  a terminal or a pipe. It now quits at once, on Linux and Windows. Its status line now reaches
  the window title under X11 too ("Shattered Stars - map 18 | ...").

## Updating from 0.02.00

Unpack the new package and use it in place of the old one. Settings, memory cards and mods live
in your user folder, so they stay as they are. If you keep your disc images in the old
package's `discs` folder, move them across.

**Seeds and runs in progress:**

| Seed made with | On Shattered Stars 0.02.00 | On Shattered Stars 0.02.50 |
|---|---|---|
| so2.apworld 0.1.0 / 0.1.1 | plays | plays, with this release's fixes |
| so2.apworld 0.2.0 | refused ("unsupported version"), or plays without some of its settings | plays |

- **You don't need to generate your seed again.** A 0.1.1 seed keeps working and gains the fixes
  above: the Lacour soft-lock, the shop purchases, the Cave of Trials chests. Generate with 0.2.0
  only for the new options. (Open-world seeds from 0.1.0 still have that version's logic errors:
  0.02.00's advice to regenerate them with 0.1.1 or newer stands.)
- **A run in progress carries on.** The first time 0.02.50 starts a seed, it copies that seed's
  saves from your memory card onto the seed's own card, once. Your own card is left as it is.
- **0.2.0 seeds need the new port.** Shattered Stars 0.02.50 reads every seed so2.apworld 0.1.0,
  0.1.1 and 0.2.0 make. Everyone playing Star Ocean in a 0.2.0 multiworld needs 0.02.50.
- **What a 0.1.x seed plays differently on 0.02.50:** a seed made for one hero's story skips the
  hero select instead of holding the cursor; in open world, new members join at the party's
  level, and your Formation leader walks the field; and the fixes above.

## Installing

1. Unpack the package for your platform anywhere.
2. Run the setup program: `setup.exe` on Windows, `./shattered-stars-setup` on Linux.
3. On the **Discs** page, choose Disc 1 and Disc 2 with **Browse...**, or put your images in
   the `discs` folder next to the game and press **Auto-detect**. Setup checks each image and
   says which disc and release it is.
4. Press **Save & Play**.

After that, start the game directly with `shattered-stars.exe` or `./shattered-stars`.

Full details: [Installing and Setup](https://github.com/IronJade/Shattered-Stars/wiki/Installing-and-Setup)
and [Playing](https://github.com/IronJade/Shattered-Stars/wiki/Playing) on the wiki.

## Playing with Archipelago

Shattered Stars has the [Archipelago](https://archipelago.gg) client built into the game — no
separate client program is needed. This world is custom and **not yet part of Archipelago
itself**, so it ships here as a separate `.apworld`:

1. Copy `so2.apworld` into the `custom_worlds` folder of **Archipelago 0.6.8 or newer**,
   replacing the old one. Keep only one copy: Archipelago refuses to load the same game twice.
2. Make your options YAML, either with the Archipelago Launcher's **Options Creator** or by
   hand, starting from `Star.Ocean.The.Second.Story.yaml`.
3. Generate as usual. Archipelago writes `AP_<number>.zip` into its `output` folder.
4. In the setup program's **Archipelago** page, press **Browse...** and choose that archive (or
   a loose `.apso2` seed file), pick your slot if it asks, and enter the room's address, your
   slot name and the password. Press **Save & Play**; the game connects as it starts.

To play a seed on your own, leave the address empty. A seed works with the discs of any
supported release, so the players of one multiworld can each use their own.

Details, every option, and troubleshooting: [Archipelago](https://github.com/IronJade/Shattered-Stars/wiki/Archipelago)
on the wiki.

## Known limitations

- **Old option names.** YAML files written for 0.1.x that use `protagonist` or
  `drops_as_checks` need those keys renamed (see [New options](#new-options)).
- **Hub mode leaves a few things out:** the story's scenes outside the boss chambers, the early gifts,
  the Lacour tournament, the fights the story lets you lose, the Portal of Sealing, the
  Nightmare, Flarelizard and Ghost fights, and the arena's prize. Scenes where the story splits
  the party are fought with the whole party.
- **The text the port adds for Archipelago is in English** on every release: the messages, the
  hub's questions, Port Hilton's captain, and the short descriptions on the character select.
  French and German Archipelago messages use the game's own font, so a symbol it lacks in a
  player or item name shows as a similar character, or a space. In French and German shops, a
  very long item name may run under the Archipelago logo.
- **Excluding both Rena and Noel** with `exclude_characters` can leave an open-world party with no
  healer.
- **Your leader on the field** gives way to the hero in a few places: a dash's afterimages show
  the hero, and so does a long scene until free play returns.
- **Port Hilton's captain:** taking his Hoffmann Ruins voyage in chapter 23 skips the soldier's
  lines of that first trip.
- **With skillsanity**, the Cave of Trials' learn spots still say the move was learned; the move
  itself arrives as its item.
- **Instant text and auto-advance** are set in setup only, not from inside the game.
- **Japanese discs aren't supported yet.** Setup recognises them and says so.
- **The PAL versions' 50 Hz timing isn't reproduced, by design.** European, French and German
  discs play on the North American game logic at 60 Hz, with their own text.
- **Saves stay with their release.** Importing a save from another release isn't offered yet.
- **Compressed disc formats** (CHD, PBP) are not accepted yet.
- **No macOS build.**
- **The Archipelago world is not yet in Archipelago itself.** Until it's merged upstream, get
  `so2.apworld` and its template from this project's releases.

See [Bugs Fixed and Quirks Kept](https://github.com/IronJade/Shattered-Stars/wiki/Bugs-Fixed-and-Quirks-Kept)
for what was changed from the original and what was deliberately left as it was.
