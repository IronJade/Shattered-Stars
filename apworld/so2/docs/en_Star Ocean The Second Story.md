# Star Ocean: The Second Story

## What does randomization do to this game?

The chests and the gifts that give items or Fol hold items from the multiworld. The story's
key items stay where they are, so the story always goes on.

## What is the goal?

Defeat Indalecio, the final boss in Fienal.

## Which locations can be missed?

Some locations close for good: a chapter window, Clik before its destruction, Private
Actions, scenes with an optional party member. By default they hold only filler
(`missable_locations`). Expel is not lost at the disc switch: after the Final Save Point in
Fienal, the old man in Fun City's arena takes you to Virtual Expel.
