Hearn's store sells Resurrection Bottles
========================================

A sample mod for Shattered Stars, made with tools/so2_mod.py:

    so2_mod.py shop DISC 1 add 0x115 -o stock.patch

It appends item 0x115 (Resurrection Bottle) to the end of shop 1's stock, the general store
in Arlia. The other items stay where they are; the price is the item's own (3600 Fol).
